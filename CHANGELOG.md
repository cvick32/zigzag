# Changelog

## 0.1.2 — 2026-11-12

- Route travel stitches through fills and beneath later stitching to reduce
  visible travel and unnecessary thread cuts.
- Sew each separate part of a fill with its underlay and top stitching together.
- Add a Connect slider with cut counts and canvas highlights, including gaps
  between parts of the same object.
- Tie off and tie in every Skitch thread cut, including short trimmed moves.
- Enable needle controls and timeline scrubbing when paused with the Skitch's
  physical Start/Stop button.
